The Foundation is the canonical name for the unified body of the Seven Churches of Áltharós — the Æcclesiæ. This page is its civic and corporate face: the institutional record of the corporation that holds the Æcclesiæ in its public form. The doctrine itself resides at altharos.church.

▲The Æcclesiæ — The Foundationi

Within the canon of Áltharós, The Foundation denotes the Æcclesiæ — the seven churches of the religion taken together as one living body. Caelánis, Glóssa, Áilithrá, Devotia, Sylvandrëa, Druúnara, and Aedhrómach compose the unified doctrine, the structure that the Aspirant ascends, and the body the Endtimes procession assembles.

Those structures are matters of doctrine. They are documented in full at altharos.church, where the Æcclesiæ live in their religious form. This section of esotericTradition.com documents the same body in its civic form — as the corporation that holds it, the office that stewards it, the personnel who carry it, and the legal and institutional facts under which the work is conducted.

▲Statement of Purposeii

Esoteric Tradition exists to produce, preserve, and transmit work attentive to the severe, the liminal, and the quietly devotional — not as style, but as method. Our mandate is generational. We build cultural, educational, and material infrastructure intended to outlast its founders and to remain serviceable when the present age has passed.

We are the cultural and corporate arm of the Æcclesiæ. The Foundation does not require assent to the religion's doctrine of those who engage with our public work; it requires only that those engaging with us do so in earnest.

▲The Office of the First Prestigeiii

The corporation is presently held in trust by the First Prestige, in whose office editorial authority, doctrinal stewardship, and the conduct of religious services reside. The First Prestige is the founder and currently the sole permanent officer of the corporation; succession will be addressed by the Foundation when the institution can support it.

▲Code of Conductiv

We extend our work in good faith. We do not engage in proselytism, in coercion, or in any practice that asks of those we serve more than they have freely chosen to give. Our spiritual and counseling services are conducted in confidence and within the limits of our competence; matters beyond that competence are referred to those qualified to attend to them.

We require of those we engage — collaborators, correspondents, those entering our care — only that they treat the work and the people of Esoteric Tradition with the same seriousness we extend to them. The full code of conduct, as it applies to commissions, services, and ministry, is held in a separate document, available on request to the Office of the First Prestige.

▲The full Code of Conduct will be published in this section in MMXXVI. Until then, the principles above govern.

▲Legal Statusvi

Esoteric Tradition is constituted as a creative society and emergent religious corporation. We pursue formal religious recognition where it is conducive to the long horizon of the work; we do not pursue recognition where the cost of that recognition compromises the integrity of the institution. The current state of recognition, in any jurisdiction, is documented in the Annual Report.

Athenæum

of Esoteric Tradition

Music · Art · Literary · Film · Services

The Athenæum is the standing cultural archive of the corporation — every project, recording, performance, publication, and work-for-hire ever issued under the imprint of Esoteric Tradition, indexed by year and by work. Recordings, books, merchandise, and physical publications presently available may be acquired directly through it.

▲Enter the Athenæum↗
The Athenæum holds the cultural and creative output of Esoteric Tradition across five disciplines — Music, Art, Literary, Film, and Services. Each discipline is conducted under the same editorial standard, and each is indexed in the archive on the same continual record. The summary below describes the current shape of the work; the full catalogue lives in the archive itself.

▲Musici

The musical catalogue is the largest body of work held in the Athenæum and the principal mode through which the corporation has, to date, transmitted its concerns. Twenty years of recordings span blackened death metal, doom, dark ambient, industrial, and gothic country, organised under the various project banners of Matron Thorn and standing collaborators. Releases are issued through the imprint and, where appropriate, through external labels of standing — among them I, Voidhanger Records, Debemur Morti Productions, 20 Buck Spin, ATMF, and Hells Headbangers.

The current release imprint of the Athenæum is Nightmare Fuel Refinery. Distribution and direct retail are conducted through the archive; full digital catalogues for each project are maintained on Bandcamp and indexed from the project pages within the Athenæum.

▲Artii

The Athenæum holds the visual catalogue of the corporation — cover artwork, photography, ritual imagery, and the graphic identity work that accompanies each release. The archive treats visual works as primary documents rather than promotional accessories, and indexes them alongside the recordings they accompany. Original works and limited prints are made available through the archive when stock conditions allow.

▲Literaryiii

The literary holdings of the Athenæum comprise poetry, fiction, essays, and the periodicals issued under our imprint. Published works to date include Serial Killer Ideology (poetry and visual art, under the pen name Matron Thorn) and The Sainthood — Book One (a cyberpunk novel by R. Christopher Jordan, the first volume of a planned trilogy). Both titles exist within a shared fictional universe held by the corporation.

Forthcoming literary works include the Áltharós Oraclæum manuscript, Spiritual Deathtrip, The Darkwander, and Prestige of the Last Outlaw. Editorial direction for the literary catalogue is held jointly by the Athenæum and by the Press, the publishing imprint of the corporation, which oversees scholarly editions and the periodicals.

▲Filmiv

The Athenæum's film holdings comprise music videos, documentary recordings of live performance and ritual, and longer-form work conducted in collaboration with external partners. Selected installations and screen works originating from the corporation — among them The Darkwander, The Alley, and the HellVoid sequence of the Sedition Institute — are presented through the archive as standing online exhibitions. Long-form film and television projects are developed in partnership with qualified production houses.

▲Servicesv

The Athenæum offers professional creative services drawn from the same operational infrastructure that produces our own catalogue — mixing, mastering, production, scoring for film and screen, design and art direction, and limited remote-recording engagements. Services are conducted from The Parish, the recording and production facility of the corporation, and are offered to outside artists, labels, and producers on a project basis.

Inquiries regarding the engagement of Athenæum services may be directed to the Office of the First Prestige via the Correspondence section of this site, or addressed to the imprint directly. Engagements are accepted at the discretion of the Office and conducted under the same standards of seriousness and confidentiality that govern our own work.

▲Editorial Standardi

We publish work that does not need to be published quickly, by writers who are willing to be edited, on subjects that warrant the page they will occupy. We do not run a list defined by genre; we run a list defined by seriousness. The catalogue will accumulate slowly and survive long.

Books issued under this imprint are produced to a single standard regardless of subject — typeset for the eye, printed for the hand, bound for the long shelf. The same applies to digital editions: every title we issue digitally is offered in formats fit for archival reading, not consumed reading.

Press inquiries — for review copies, for licensing, for catalogue collaboration — may be directed to the Office of the First Prestige via the Correspondence page.

The Institute is the educational arm of Esoteric Tradition. We publish public lectures, recordings, and open-access scholarship; in the long horizon, we intend to establish a college-level academy under our standard.

▲Mandatei

Our mandate is to make available, freely or at modest cost, the scholarship and instruction we are competent to offer. The Institute is not a church school. It is a public-facing educational arm of a corporation that takes seriously its obligation to teach what it knows, to those who have asked.

The first programmes of the Institute will be lectures and recorded series, drawn from the standing competence of the corporation: theology and the constructed languages of the tradition, religious music and its history, devotional and ritual literature, and the editorial craft as it is practised here.

▲Programmes in Formationii

The inaugural programme of the Institute is in preparation. Until the first lectures are issued, the Institute remains in formation, and this section serves as a register of what is forthcoming.

  • Recorded scholarshipLong-form recordings on subjects within the standing competence of the corporation.Format: Audio · As issued

▲Public lectures and recordings will be listed on this page as they are issued. Subscription and access conditions will be published with each programme.

▲Standard of Instructioniii

We teach within our competence and decline to teach beyond it. Where a subject is part of the tradition but is held by another scholar more qualified to teach it, we will commission that scholar; where no qualified scholar exists, we will defer the subject until one is available. The Institute will not run a course for the sake of the course.

Recordings are issued in their entirety. We do not sell teaching in fragments designed to require further purchase. A lecture is a lecture; a programme is a programme; the conditions of access to each are stated plainly.

VMinistry

Ministry is the civic and care arm of Esoteric Tradition. At present we extend religious services and spiritual counseling by appointment; in the long horizon we will operate clinical, recovery, and protective work in line with our doctrine of care.

▲The Doctrine of Carei

We hold that care, when extended in good faith and within competence, is one of the few things a religious institution owes the world without condition. Our care is offered on the basis of need, not affiliation; we do not require those we attend to share our doctrine, our practice, or our ends.

The Ministry will, over time, build the institutions that this principle requires of us. The work begins with what we are competent to do today and extends from there as the corporation grows.

▲Services Currently Extendedii

At present, the Ministry extends two services: religious services and spiritual counseling, both by appointment with the Office of the First Prestige. Both are conducted in confidence, within the limits of our competence, and at no cost or honorarium that exceeds reasonable expense.

  • Religious servicesRites of passage, dedication, blessing, and remembrance. Conducted in person where possible; remotely where required.By appointment · Counsel: Rev. Matron Thorn
  • Spiritual counselingConfidential consultation on matters of devotion, vocation, and discernment. Not a substitute for clinical mental health care.By appointment · Confidence assured
  • ReferralWhere a matter exceeds our competence, we will refer to those qualified to attend to it. We do not retain those we cannot serve.As required

▲The Care Arm — In Formationiii

The wider care arm of the Ministry is in formation. The work we intend to undertake, as the corporation gains the resources to undertake it, includes addiction recovery, mental-health support, protection of women and children at risk, hospice and end-of-life care, and clinical work conducted in partnership with qualified practitioners.

We do not at present claim to operate any of these services. We state the intent here so that those who would help us build them, and those who might one day be served by them, can see the shape of what we mean to do.

▲The Ministry's published scope of services and conditions of access will be set out in this section as each programme becomes operative.

▲Honoraria & Conditionsiv

Religious services and spiritual counseling are offered without fee. Where travel or material costs are required, we ask that those costs be covered; where the person seeking service cannot meet them, we will absorb them or decline the service rather than make it conditional. The schedule of reasonable expenses, and the conditions under which we may decline a request, will be published with the full Code of Conduct in MMXXVI.

Dispatches is our long-form writing — the editorial archive of Esoteric Tradition. We issue a monthly essay; from time to time, bulletins from the Foundation, the Athenæum, the Press, the Institute, and the Ministry as occasion warrants.

▲Editorial Cadencei

We issue one essay per month. The essay does not chase the news of the day; it addresses what the moment requires of an institution that thinks in decades. A subject is set down when it is ready; a piece is published when it is finished.

Bulletins from the other arms of the corporation are issued on no schedule. They appear when there is occasion: the inaugural issue of an imprint, the opening of a programme, a public statement on a matter on which we have been asked to speak.

▲The Archiveii

Every essay and bulletin we issue is held here in perpetuity, dated and unaltered. We may issue corrections; we do not issue revisions in silence. The archive is the institutional record of what we have said.

▲Subscriptioniii

Dispatches will be available to read at no cost on this page. A modest subscription will be offered to those who wish to support the editorial work; subscribers will receive each issue by correspondence on the day of publication. The subscription is not a paywall — the archive remains open.

Subscription details will be set out here when the inaugural essay is issued. In the meantime, those who wish to be notified when Dispatches goes live may write to the Office of the First Prestige via the Correspondence page.

Áltharós

Esoteric Tradition is the cultural and corporate arm of a religious society called Áltharós. Its doctrine, its sevenfold Æcclesiæ, and its inner life reside elsewhere.

▲Pass to altharos.church↗
▲External · A separate domain▲

▲What lies beyond this gateway▲

altharos.church is the living face of the religion. There are gathered: the canonical writings of the tradition, the sevenfold structure of the Æcclesiæ, and the inner life of the community.

It is a separate place, kept under separate authority. We name it here so that those who know what they are looking for can find it; we keep it elsewhere so that those who are not looking for it are not asked to consider it.

Passage beyond is by qualification. The site itself is the qualifier.
