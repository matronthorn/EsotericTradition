Ævangelist
Blackened Death Metal / Avant-Garde  ·  Fort Lauderdale, Florida — Now Tampere, Finland  ·  Est. 2010
Matron Thorn — All instruments, guitars, bass, keyboards
Stéphane Gerbaud — Vocals, lyrics
M. Garcia — Drums & percussion
Founded in 2010 in Fort Lauderdale, Florida and now based in Tampere, Finland, Ævangelist is the foremost musical expression of the Athenaeum — a blackened death metal entity operating at the intersection of extreme music and esoteric transmission. All instrumentation is the work of Matron Thorn, with vocals and lyrics by Stéphane Gerbaud and drums by M. Garcia. The sound of Ævangelist resists classification: dissonant, cavernous, and layered with industrial noise and dark ambient textures, it functions less as conventional music than as an aural rite — a sustained frequency intended to disturb the boundary between the heard and the felt. Previous releases through I, Voidhanger Records (1st), Debemur Morti Productions (1st), Debemur Morti Productions (2nd), 20 Buck Spin, and I, Voidhanger Records (2nd). Current release imprint: Nightmare Fuel Refinery.

Benighted in Sodom
Black Metal / Shoegaze / Darkwave / Doom / Grunge / Industrial  ·  Tampere, Finland  ·  Est. 2006
Matron Thorn — Everything
The oldest active project of Rev. Matron Thorn, Benighted in Sodom has existed since 2006 as an unmediated interior voice — black metal filtered through shoegaze, darkwave, doom, goth, grunge, and industrial, recorded at The Parish in Tampere. With over 34 releases across full-lengths, EPs, and collaborations, it is among the most prolific solo catalogues in extreme music. Released through I, Voidhanger Records and self-released. The most recent full-length, Fringes of Destiny (2025), continues the trajectory of restless, melancholic exploration.

Skrying Mirror
Industrial-Tinged Black / Death Metal  ·  Tampere, Finland  ·  Est. 2021
Matron Thorn — Guitars, bass, synthetics
Kevin Yearout — Vocals, lyrics
M. Garcia — Drums
Skrying Mirror emerged in 2021 as a collaborative convergence — Matron Thorn on guitars, bass, and synthetics, Kevin Yearout on vocals and lyrics, and M. Garcia on drums. The debut EP Garden of Ecstatic Violence (2021) announced the project's intent: industrial-inflected blackened death metal channelling the accumulated experience of Ævangelist's sonic world into a more compressed and violent form. The debut full-length Omnimalevolence (I, Voidhanger Records, 2023) expanded the vision to its full scope — a dense, asphyxiating work widely regarded as among the year's most significant extreme metal releases.

    Áltharós

    Áltharós
    Parish of Esoteric Tradition

    Áltharós is a New Age religion founded in the year 2026 by Rev. Matron Thorn, First Prestige of Áltharós. It is composed of The Foundation, seven constituent Churches known collectively as the Æcclesiæ of Áltharós, and a canon — the Oraclæum — from which The Foundation takes its doctrine. Religious Corporation Esoteric Tradition is the planetary interface to the Aëthÿrinn.

    Áltharós does not address the world through invitation. The existence of this online portal is a matter of public record; its doctrine is not a matter of public elaboration. The pages that follow disclose what is appropriate and permissible for the civic presence of Esoteric Tradition, and by extension, The Foundation. As well, there is no tangible temple of Áltharós; aspirants of relative proximity may be gathered in the Midnight Sanctum under special circumstances. Nothing more.

    Those whose path has already brought them here in a manner they recognize may address the Office of the First Prestige in writing. All other inquiries are referred to the same.

    Rev. Matron ThornFirst Prestige of Áltharós

    The Foundation is the institutional body of Áltharós. It comprises seven constituent Churches, the Æcclesiæ, the canonical text of the Oraclæum, a para-governing office, and the observances that bind them. Its civic name is the Parish of Esoteric Tradition. Its doctrinal name is Áltharós.

    The Foundation holds that authority proceeds from the canon. It does not proceed from the institution to the canon. For this reason, the office of the First Prestige is the canon's present custodian rather than its origin.

    The present First Prestige is Rev. Matron Thorn, by whose hand the canon was set down and under whose authority the Foundation enters the period of canonical consolidation.

    The Foundation is in the process of civil registration under the statutes of the jurisdictions appropriate to its governance. The Parish of Esoteric Tradition is the operational name under which this work proceeds. Its seat is in Finland.

    Office of the First Prestige
    Parish of Esoteric Tradition
    Finland  ·  MMXXVI

    The canonical scripture of Áltharós is the Oraclæum. It is the Nescience of the First Prestige: the delivery of illumination to those who would come after. The manuscript is the authoritative statement of the Foundation's theology, cosmology, and observance.

    The Oraclæum exists, in its authoritative form, as three leather-bound manuscripts. These are the canonical physical objects of the Foundation. No digital, printed, or commercial edition supersedes them.

    The Oraclæum is not reproduced in public. Passages are not published, excerpted for promotion, or presented outside the governance of the Foundation. This is a doctrinal matter, not a matter of policy — the canon addresses those whom it addresses in the condition in which they are addressed, and unsanctioned reproduction is understood to falsify it. The public editions sanctioned by the Office of the First Prestige are not exceptions to this but instruments of it: governed transmissions prepared, under the Office's authority, for those who seek them.

    The canon is in a period of consolidation. Its structure is complete; certain sections remain in the process of being finalized under the authority of the First Prestige.

    The Foundation is composed of seven Churches. Each is a domain of labor and a register of the canon's theology. The civic descriptions given here are the names under which the Churches are referred to outside their doctrinal context.

    • I.Áilithrá Church of Divination
    • II.Sylvandrëa Church of Verdant Equilibrium
    • III.Glóssa Church of Lexicon
    • IV.Aedhrómach Church of Sovereign Order
    • V.Caelánis Church of Spirit, Craft, and Materia
    • VI.Druúnara Church of Clairvoyance
    • VII.Æolandéir Church of the Ethereal

    The Churches are not congregations in the ordinary civic sense. Each is defined by a domain within the canon's theology and by the labors that proceed from it. Further description of those labors belongs to the canon.

    The Foundation observes seven tenets — the Triskelion of Sevens — under which the Churches operate in common. The tenets are named below without elaboration; their content is a matter of the canon.

    • Clandestia
    • Divinatria
    • Devotia
    • Endura
    • Ekstasiia
    • Abscondia
    • Catharsia

    Áltharós understands itself to be in a definite period of its own history. The period now in progress is the period of canonical consolidation: the period during which the Oraclæum is finalized in its authoritative form, during which the Foundation is entered into civil registration, and during which the Office of the First Prestige stands alone as the governing seat.

    The periods that will follow are matters of the canon. They are not advertised as a schedule or a calendar. The Foundation acknowledges only the present period, and the horizon of its own eschatology, which is named in the canon and not here.

    The Foundation does not receive aspirants to the religion in the ordinary civic manner during this period. The reasons for this belong to the doctrine. A petition to the Office remains open to those with cause.

    Dated in the year of the Foundation: MMXXVI.

    The Office of the First Prestige is the governing seat of the Foundation and its sole addressable authority during the present period. All inquiries — civic, scholarly, doctrinal, personal — are received at the Office.

    Those who address the Office are asked to observe the conventions of the Foundation: petitions are received, not requested of; they are reviewed in silence; a response is not guaranteed and is not owed. The Office does not respond to petitions whose character falls outside the concerns of Áltharós.

    The Office of the First Prestige does not solicit or receive donations, and the Office itself provides no services; creative commissions and the work of ministry are conducted by the Parish, under the Athenæum. The Office is addressed for matters of the Foundation alone.
