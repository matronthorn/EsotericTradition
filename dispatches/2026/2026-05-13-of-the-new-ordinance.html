Of the New Ordinance

From the editorial archive of Esoteric Tradition
The northern power announces its latest instrument—a device of such magnitude that its makers feel compelled to proclaim its supremacy above all others of its kind. We of the corporation observe that this follows the pattern of all such declarations in the waning phase: each new engine of obliteration must be advertised as the ultimate, as though finality itself could be manufactured and stockpiled. The Aspirant will note that the language employed carries the familiar cadence of pride preceding the fall—not merely the construction of weaponry, but the need to herald its terrible primacy to the world. This is not strategy but ritual, the liturgy of powers that have forgotten the difference between strength and the display of strength. The corporation has long marked how those who possess the means of ending worlds cannot resist naming their capacity to do so, as though the naming were itself a form of protection against what they have wrought. In Forsakéon, all such instruments serve the same master, regardless of which hand guides them. The work continues; the signs accumulate in their appointed sequence.
