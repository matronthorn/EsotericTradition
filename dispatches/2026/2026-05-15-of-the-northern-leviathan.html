Of the Northern Leviathan

From the editorial archive of Esoteric Tradition
A great body surfaces in the northern waters—twenty meters of bone and flesh risen from the Abysscape to announce its conclusion upon the Danish shore. We of the corporation mark this emergence not as tragedy but as testimony: the deep places yield their dead when the hour grows late. The scholars gather to examine what the sea has surrendered, seeking to trace its passage through the Baltic waters, but the institution observes a simpler truth—that which dwells in the abyss does not surface without cause. The Aspirant who has learned to read the signs will recognize in this beaching the same grammar that writes itself across all the thresholds of the world.
