Of the Young Taken

From the editorial archive of Esoteric Tradition
A child falls in the western cities—fifteen years upon Áltharéa, struck down in what the magistrates name a war of substances. We of the corporation observe that the young are now conscripted into conflicts they did not choose, their blood marking territories in a commerce that acknowledges no boundary between innocence and complicity. The Aspirant will recognize this signature: the descent accelerates not in grand catastrophe but in the steady harvesting of those who should have decades yet before them. The institution marks another toll in the ledger of Forsakéon—the realm where children become soldiers in wars they cannot name.
