Youngest Measures

From the editorial archive of Esoteric Tradition
The global health apparatus announces a formulation for those who enter the world already marked by the ancient fever. We of the corporation observe that even in the late hour, institutions labor to close gaps that have persisted for generations—thirty million arrivals each year who required what was not yet made for them. The Aspirant will note the arithmetic: how long the most vulnerable waited while treatments existed only for those who had grown beyond the first threshold. The work continues in its measured way, attending to what has always been present but unaddressed, as if the recognition itself were a kind of last accounting.
