The Southern Contagion

From the editorial archive of Esoteric Tradition
The corporation has received word from the health ministries of the world—progress is announced, yet the count remains: over a million souls claimed annually by the hepatic malady, with thousands of new transmissions each day. We of the Æcclesiæ observe that even their measured gains reveal the deeper pattern: the human vessel continues its long compromise with what dwells in blood and liver, and the institutions tasked with its preservation speak now in the careful language of managed decline. Targets are set for distant years; achievements are tallied against projections rather than against the cessation of suffering. The Aspirant will recognize in these dispatches the same ledger that tracks all earthly campaigns—incremental victories counted while the tide itself advances.
