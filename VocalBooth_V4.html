VocalBooth

Sing your clean vocals on your phone against the demo. One take, wear headphones.

1. Load the project

The .rpp from VigilMaster, or the Vocal Booth file made in the Lyrics tab for an older project (either carries every line, its time, key and words). A .vmlyr lyrics project also works on its own: its lines are timed from bars and tempo, counted from the start of each song, so use demos that begin at the start of the song. Optional: the album lyrics file (.txt from the Lyrics tab, or .vmlyr) to use your latest words.

2. Pick a song

 
Level 0 dB

Wear wired headphones. The phone's own microphone can be used while headphones play the demo; if your headphones have a mic, pick whichever you want above. Bluetooth headphones add delay and often switch the mic to a poor mode. On Android, wired earphones with the phone's own mic usually give the least delay; if the take lands late, use the latency trim.

Audio starts at Latency trim (ms)

How the take reaches REAPER. Export the WAV, copy it to your Mac, drop it on Lead Vocal (record) anywhere in the project and run ParishAutoMix. The script finds where it belongs by itself.
