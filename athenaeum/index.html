Ævangelist
Blackened Death Metal / Avant-Garde  ·  Fort Lauderdale, Florida — Now Tampere, Finland  ·  Est. 2010
Matron Thorn — All instruments, guitars, bass, keyboards
Stéphane Gerbaud — Vocals, lyrics
M. Garcia — Drums & percussion
Founded in 2010 in Fort Lauderdale, Florida and now based in Tampere, Finland, Ævangelist is the foremost musical expression of the Athenaeum — a blackened death metal entity operating at the intersection of extreme music and esoteric transmission. All instrumentation is the work of Matron Thorn, with vocals and lyrics by Stéphane Gerbaud and drums by M. Garcia. The sound of Ævangelist resists classification: dissonant, cavernous, and layered with industrial noise and dark ambient textures, it functions less as conventional music than as an aural rite — a sustained frequency intended to disturb the boundary between the heard and the felt. Previous releases through I, Voidhanger Records (1st), Debemur Morti Productions (1st), Debemur Morti Productions (2nd), 20 Buck Spin, and I, Voidhanger Records (2nd). Current release imprint: Nightmare Fuel Refinery.

Black Metal / Shoegaze / Darkwave / Doom / Grunge / Industrial  ·  Tampere, Finland  ·  Est. 2006

The oldest active project of Rev. With over 34 releases across full-lengths, EPs, and collaborations, it is among the most prolific solo catalogues in extreme music. Released through I, Voidhanger Records and self-released. The most recent full-length, Fringes of Destiny (2025), continues the trajectory of restless, melancholic exploration.

Skrying Mirror
Industrial-Tinged Black / Death Metal  ·  Tampere, Finland  ·  Est. 2021
Matron Thorn — Guitars, bass, synthetics
Kevin Yearout — Vocals, lyrics
M. Garcia — Drums
Skrying Mirror emerged in 2021 as a collaborative convergence — Matron Thorn on guitars, bass, and synthetics, Kevin Yearout on vocals and lyrics, and M. Garcia on drums. The debut EP Garden of Ecstatic Violence (2021) announced the project's intent: industrial-inflected blackened death metal channelling the accumulated experience of Ævangelist's sonic world into a more compressed and violent form. The debut full-length Omnimalevolence (I, Voidhanger Records, 2023) expanded the vision to its full scope — a dense, asphyxiating work widely regarded as among the year's most significant extreme metal releases.

Cyberpunk / Trip Hop / Ambient / Cinematic / Experimental  ·  Tampere, Finland
Matron Thorn
Rust-Colored Glasses is a project of cyberpunk conceptualism and atmospheric precision — trip hop, ambient, cinematic, and experimental in orientation. The project is conceptually based on the cyberpunk novel trilogy The Sainthood by Rev. Matron Thorn. The full-length S T A S I S : F L U X (2021) traces an extended emotional and sonic arc across 13 tracks, occupying a space between defined categories — neither black metal nor ambient nor post-rock, but drawing from all three while belonging to none.

California Blood Ritual
Southern Black / Death Metal / Horror Punk / Occult  ·  Tampere, Finland
Matron Thorn — All instruments, vocals
Manuel Garcia — Drums
California Blood Ritual is the work of Matron Thorn on all instruments and vocals and Manuel Garcia on drums — Southern black and death metal with horror punk and occult undertones. The name invokes a specific geography: the American West Coast as a site of mythologized darkness, cult violence, and the collapse of utopian ambition into something far more sinister. Personal history transformed into ritual sound.

Ωblivion Gate
Industrial / Darkwave / Doom  ·  Tampere, Finland
Matron Thorn
Ωblivion Gate stands at the threshold of ending — an industrial, darkwave, and doom project whose name announces its concern: the passage through which the self, the world, and the known dissolve. Debut album "Wisdom of the Grave" was released on ATMF in 2019. Second album "Flowers in the Wreckage of Dreams" followed. Most recent album "Thrill Kill Noir" was released by Esoteric Tradition.

Vagrant Starscape
Ethereal Darkwave / New Age  ·  Portland, Oregon / Tampere, Finland
Rev. Matron Thorn
Ethereal Darkwave and New Age music theologically canon to the cosmological framework of Áltharós. Vagrant Starscape exists at the outer edge of the Athenaeum — not extreme metal but a sustained ambient drift, devotional and vast, shaped by the same interior architecture that produces the rest of Rev.
